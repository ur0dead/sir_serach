Automation Resources for Legal and Business Teams

Repetitive work such as reading documents, copying data between systems and checking public records eats up hours that teams could spend on higher-value tasks. Modern automation, from simple scripts to AI-assisted assistants, can take a large share of that load. This page collects the guides and services we get asked about most.

Where to start

If you are new to automation, begin by listing the tasks your team repeats every week. The best candidates are rule-based, high-volume and low-risk. Once you have that list, the resources below show how similar problems are solved in practice.

Choosing the right approach

Not every problem needs AI. Fixed formats and predictable rules are usually best handled with straightforward scripts, which are cheaper and easier to maintain. Reserve language models for tasks involving messy text, where flexibility matters more than strict rules. Whichever route you take, test on real samples early and keep a human in the loop for anything high-stakes.
