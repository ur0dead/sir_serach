Look up voter SIR records on WhatsApp

SIR SEARCH is a WhatsApp-based service that looks up voter SIR (electoral roll) records for you. Send us a message with your EPIC / voter ID number, or your full name and a relative's name (such as your father or husband), and we reply with the matching electoral roll entry — including name, voter ID, polling station, and assembly constituency details.

Everything happens right inside WhatsApp. There is no app to install and no account to create. Save our WhatsApp number, send your search details, pay the small fee through the UPI QR code we send you in chat, and receive your results in the same conversation.

Privacy policy

SIR SEARCH respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, used to identify your account and track search credits.
  • Your search queries — this includes the EPIC / voter ID number, or the name and relative's name you send us to perform a lookup.
  • Payment confirmations received via UPI, used to credit searches to your number.

How we use your information

  • To perform the voter SIR (electoral roll) lookup you requested.
  • To track paid search credits associated with your WhatsApp number.
  • To respond to your messages and provide customer support.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your information for marketing or advertising.
  • We do not store your search results beyond the time needed to deliver them to you in chat.

Data retention

We retain your phone number and a record of your credits only for as long as needed to provide the service. Search query details are used to perform the lookup and are not kept longer than necessary.

Contact for privacy questions

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, write to us at ak1256573@gmail.com.

Refund policy

Search credits are consumed on a per-search basis once a lookup is successfully completed and the result is delivered to you in WhatsApp. Because of this, refunds are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

When a refund may be given

  • If a search fails and no result is delivered, the credit for that search is not consumed and remains available to you.
  • If you paid for credits but they were not added to your number due to a technical error on our side, we will add the missing credits or refund the payment.
  • If you are unable to use your credits due to a service outage on our end, contact us and we will resolve it.

When a refund is not applicable

  • If a search was completed and the result was delivered, the credit used is non-refundable.
  • If no matching electoral roll record is found for your query, the search was still performed and the credit is used.

How to request a refund

To request a refund, email us at ak1256573@gmail.com with your WhatsApp number and a description of the issue. We review each request individually and respond within a reasonable timeframe.
